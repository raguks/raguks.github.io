# Dharma | ಧರ್ಮ | Duties

Dharma examines what should guide individuals and institutions, what responsibilities arise from power and participation, and what enables society to endure.

This domain includes:

- ethics and responsibility;
- leadership and professional conduct;
- institutions and accountability;
- society and civic life;
- governance and public policy;
- law and justice;
- duties and rights;
- education as a social responsibility;
- cultural continuity;
- environmental responsibility;
- and the principles that sustain collective life.

## Nīti within Dharma

Nīti is the practical and strategic dimension of Dharma, not a separate publication domain.

It includes:

- leadership judgment;
- policy and statecraft;
- decision-making under constraints;
- professional conduct;
- negotiation;
- institutional strategy;
- and the practical application of principles under imperfect conditions.

Leadership belongs under Dharma when the central question concerns conduct, responsibility, power, institutions, society, or public life.

Leadership concerned mainly with professional growth, organizational performance, management, or enterprise value may belong under Artha.

Use `Niti` as a tag when practical judgment is an important secondary dimension.
