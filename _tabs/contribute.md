# Contribute to New Roots

## Unfamiliar Ideas. Serious Inquiry.

New Roots is being developed as an independent home for essays, frameworks, and ideas that deserve careful development and examination.

Formal submissions are not yet open. In the future, New Roots may invite contributions across four domains.

### Artha | ಅರ್ಥ

Professional life, organizations, enterprise, management, economics, careers, and value creation.

### Tantra | ತಂತ್ರ

Engineering, technology, software, systems, architecture, computation, artificial intelligence, and technical methods.

### Dharma | ಧರ್ಮ

Leadership, conduct, institutions, society, governance, justice, responsibility, culture, and public reasoning.

Nīti is treated as an applied dimension of Dharma rather than as a separate publication domain.

### Jñāna | ಜ್ಞಾನ

Knowledge, science, mathematics, philosophy, history, language, civilization, traditional knowledge, and intellectual inquiry.

## What Makes a Strong Contribution?

A contribution should do more than provide commentary.

A strong submission should accomplish at least one of the following:

- define an important problem more precisely;
- question a foundational assumption;
- construct a useful framework;
- connect fields usually examined separately;
- document a meaningful practical experience;
- recover and carefully interpret a neglected source;
- explain an unfamiliar idea clearly;
- or propose an alternative that can be examined, challenged, or tested.

Unfamiliarity alone is not enough.

A serious contribution must distinguish among:

- documented facts;
- personal observations;
- interpretations;
- analogies;
- hypotheses;
- proposals;
- and predictions.

Contributors should engage credible objections and identify the limits of their arguments.

## Choosing a Domain

Choose the domain based on the article's central question.

Use **Artha** when the article is mainly about value, work, enterprise, organizations, markets, careers, or resources.

Use **Tantra** when the article is mainly about engineering, technology, methods, architecture, computation, or constructed systems.

Use **Dharma** when the article is mainly about conduct, leadership responsibility, institutions, governance, ethics, society, or public life.

Use **Jñāna** when the article is mainly about knowledge, science, mathematics, philosophy, history, language, or conceptual inquiry.

Use tags to represent secondary subjects and cross-domain perspectives.

## Languages

New Roots intends to support writing in:

- English
- Kannada | ಕನ್ನಡ

Bilingual articles are welcome when both versions preserve the meaning and intellectual character of the work rather than providing a purely mechanical translation.

## Author Rights

Contributors retain copyright in their work unless a different written arrangement is explicitly agreed upon.

Publication on New Roots does not automatically grant unrestricted reuse, republication, adaptation, or commercial rights to third parties.

Detailed submission guidelines will be published when formal submissions open.
